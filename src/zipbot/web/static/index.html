Discord media downloader

Save every attachment of a Discord message at once — images, videos and any other file — as a ZIP or straight to your phone.

  1. Right-click (or long-press) a message → Apps → Download attachments.
  2. Tap Save to phone or Download ZIP in the reply that only you can see.

You can also forward messages to the bot in a DM.

Files go straight from Discord to your device; this server never stores them. Privacy · Terms
