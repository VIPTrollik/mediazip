Discord media downloader

Download every image and video of a Discord message at once — as a ZIP or straight to your device. Photos, GIFs and video clips, in one go.

Add to Discord Add to a server

Add to Discord puts it on your account, for any server or DM. Add to a server makes it available to everyone there (needs Manage Server).

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord.

How it works

  1. Pick a message

    Right-click or long-press it, then choose Apps → Download media.

  2. Download

    In the reply only you can see, tap Download all for separate files or Download ZIP for one archive.

  3. Or forward it

    Forward messages to the bot in a DM, or upload photos and videos there, and get one link for all of them.

Your images and videos go straight from Discord to your device. This server never stores them, keeps no file names in its logs, and download links are short-lived. How your data is handled
